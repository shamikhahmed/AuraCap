AuraCap Privacy

Effective 15 September 2026 · Published by Capricorn Systems, Karachi, Pakistan

Summary

AuraCap works on your device. We don't have accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and we don't sell data.

What stays on your device

  • Imported app lists and categories
  • Profiles, layouts, wallpapers and lock screen choices
  • Setup report / score results and preferences (theme, accent, reminders)
  • Optional JSON backups you create yourself

What leaves your device

ServiceWhat is sentWhyWhen
Nothing——AuraCap makes no network calls for your data. Static app files load from GitHub Pages.

Your controls

Export your data: Settings → Export & Sync. Erase all data: Settings → Danger zone → Factory reset.

Children

AuraCap is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over.

Changes

We'll update this page and its effective date if anything changes.
